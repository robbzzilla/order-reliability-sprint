# Trading platform technology

Robert Hampton is exploring software subscriptions, component licenses
and a defined technology sale for teams building trading applications.
The first step is a written fit discussion with an engineering owner
about one order-state or recovery workflow.

## Initial technical focus

The initial candidate is an Alpaca-specific order journal and recovery
workflow. Existing source records dispatch claims and observations,
retains uncertain outcomes and uses original client-order identifiers
during recovery. A missing lookup is not permission to submit another
order. Cumulative order observations do not establish individual execution
or fee detail. Other broker adapters require a separate technical scope.

Development uses disclosed AI assistance. The candidate source has been
inventoried; dependency and packaging work and an isolated demonstration
remain before a customer release. A hosted customer service and an
installable buyer package are not available yet.

## Commercial paths under consideration

| Path | What we would qualify in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Software subscription | One paid design-partner evaluation, the recurring workflow a buyer needs, and acceptance and renewal terms before a hosted service is built. |
| Component license | Selected source and documentation, included rights, the buyer's environment, and separately scoped integration and support. |
| Technology sale | A defined asset package, transferable rights, retained owner use, transition work and payment terms. |

Platform prices and deal terms are not set. The separate
[USD 2,500 engineering review](README.md) is a service offer, not a
platform license price or technology valuation.

## Start with one workflow

Please identify the workflow, broker or adapter, current software stack,
engineering owner and commissioning party. Tell us which commercial path
you are considering, the budget available for evaluation, and who would
accept the result. We can discuss permitted synthetic or redacted inputs,
expected behavior, delivery scope and payment terms in the same thread.

For a component license or asset purchase, tell us what you need
us to deliver, which rights you need, and the role that approves
the purchase.

For a paid evaluation, we agree on the expected order behavior, evidence
cutoff, fee and acceptance criteria before work begins. The report records
the order disposition supported by the evidence, what remains unresolved,
and what the reviewer should verify next. It also records whether the
workflow warrants recurring review.

Any service or integration scope must define customer data and access
boundaries. The customer retains control of its brokerage accounts,
trading decisions and deployment.
