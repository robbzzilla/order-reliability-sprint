# Open estimate review for roofing and HVAC offices

Robert Hampton offers an AI-assisted, written review of one office's
existing estimate queue. The introductory pilot is **USD 1,500 fixed**.
It helps the office review estimates that lack a recorded next step and
check the handoff from approved estimates to jobs and scheduling.

## What we review

We agree on one location, one CRM, one estimate queue and a period of up
to 60 days, including the batch size and evidence coverage. Using authorized,
redacted exports and your office's rules, we:

- Group estimate revisions so the same opportunity is not counted twice.
- Flag open estimates due for office review, while excluding opted-out,
  declined or otherwise ineligible records.
- Compare approved estimates with existing jobs and dated scheduling
  evidence, holding unclear scope or missing coverage for office verification.

## What you receive

A prioritized worklist with evidence references and reasons, two written
templates for office review, and a configuration checklist for your existing
CRM. Missing evidence is marked for verification rather than treated as
proof that work was missed.

Your office controls every customer message, scheduling decision and CRM
change. The review uses exports; it does not require a new software purchase
or promise recovered revenue.

## Check what your office can export

For an estimate follow-up review, check that the export can show the
estimate ID, current revision and status, date sent, quote value, and
the last follow-up date and whether follow-up is permitted. Use the
same anonymous estimate ID across files. If your CRM tracks the next
scheduled reminder or a responsible office role, include those fields
without personal details. A future reminder stays on the existing
schedule. When follow-up history is missing, the office verifies it
before deciding whether to contact the customer.

For an approved-estimate handoff review, also check whether the office
can identify approved estimates, match each to any existing job, and
verify whether that job has a dated visit. A blank link needs an office
check before anyone opens a job. If the job or schedule evidence is
unavailable, we can agree on an estimate-only review and leave the
handoff unverified.

## Scope and delivery

The five-business-day delivery period begins after **written scope approval
and receipt of the complete agreed inputs**. Before kickoff, we agree on the
input list, eligibility rules, coverage cutoff, reviewer, acceptance and
payment terms. A partner may commission the review for one named client
office, with the payer and responsibilities agreed in writing.

Qualification, delivery and questions stay in writing. Before transferring
redacted exports, we agree on authorized access, permitted tools and data
retention. Keep customer names, contact details, free-text notes and
credentials out of the initial inquiry.
