# Open estimate review for roofing and HVAC offices

Robert Hampton offers an AI-assisted, written review of one office's
existing estimate queue. The introductory pilot is **USD 1,500 fixed**.
It helps the office review estimates that lack a recorded next step and
check the handoff from approved estimates to jobs and scheduling.

## What we review

We agree on one location, one CRM, one estimate queue and a period of up
to 60 days, including the batch size and evidence coverage. Using authorized,
redacted exports and your office's rules, we:

- Group estimate revisions so the same opportunity is not counted twice.
- Flag open estimates due for office review, while excluding opted-out,
  declined or otherwise ineligible records.
- Compare approved estimates with existing jobs and dated scheduling
  evidence, holding unclear scope or missing coverage for office verification.

## What you receive

You receive a prioritized worklist, two written templates for office
review, and a configuration checklist for your existing CRM. Each
unresolved handoff names the office role that should act, the decision
it needs to make, and the redacted evidence behind that decision. Missing
evidence gets a verification action before the office decides what to do.

Your office controls every customer message, scheduling decision and CRM
change. The review uses exports; it does not require a new software purchase
or promise recovered revenue.

### When two work options cost the same

A separate example has a $2,600 approved estimate that includes a $400
motor option. The linked job has the same $2,600 total and a booked visit,
and lists a $400 duct option in place of the approved work. The worklist
sends the job to the office reviewer to confirm the selected option. For
this check, supply the approved revision, selected work and corresponding
job lines using the same anonymous references.

## Check what your office can export

For an estimate follow-up review, check that the export can show the
estimate ID, current revision and status, date sent, quote value, and
the last follow-up date and whether follow-up is permitted. Use the
same anonymous estimate ID across files. If your CRM tracks the next
scheduled reminder or a responsible office role, include those fields
without personal details. A future reminder stays on the existing
schedule. When follow-up history is missing, the office verifies it
before deciding whether to contact the customer.

If your office uses Jobber, open the Quotes report under Insights and
Reports. For a sent-quote batch, select **Sent within** and export only
the agreed columns. A re-sent quote may appear in the selected period while
its Sent date still shows the first send. Include the latest redacted
contact record and any scheduled customer follow-up when available. Keep
internal calendar reminders separate from outbound customer messages.
[Jobber Quotes report](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quotes-report/) · [Jobber reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/reminders-on-the-schedule/)

If your office uses ServiceTitan, check whether the Opportunity and
Estimate Follow Up report can show both estimate status and opportunity
status with the same anonymous keys. One opportunity can contain several
estimate options. If one option was sold, ask the office whether any
remaining open options are alternatives to that sale. When the statuses
disagree, ask the office to verify the accepted work, the configured sold
threshold and the job link before deciding which row needs action.
[ServiceTitan report guide](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/opportunity-and-estimate-follow-up-report) · [Roofing estimate follow-up guide](https://help.servicetitan.com/residential-s-r/docs/follow-up-on-unsold-estimates-for-roofing)

For an approved-estimate handoff review, also check whether the office
can identify approved estimates, match each to any existing job, and
verify whether that job has a dated visit. A blank link needs an office
check before anyone opens a job. If the job or schedule evidence is
unavailable, we can agree on an estimate-only review and leave the
handoff unverified.

If your office uses Housecall Pro and one approved estimate depends on
price book items, check whether you can provide the approved estimate and
the service or material reference used at approval. Use redacted item
references, units and prices. If you have only the current price book,
we can flag a difference for the office to verify. The office decides
whether any estimate needs a correction.
[Housecall Pro export guide](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/7173129-price-book-import-or-export-services-and-materials)

If your office uses Simpro, check whether you can provide the approved quote
revision, selected cost centres and the matching job under the same anonymous
references. A quote can be converted to a job before a visit is scheduled, so
include separate evidence of the dated visit. If your export cannot link
the quote to a job, the office verifies the link before opening or scheduling work.
[Simpro quote conversion guide](https://developer.simprogroup.com/apidoc/?page=07adc9c3c19b7c17e7309e86c22161a2)

## Scope and delivery

The five-business-day delivery period begins after **written scope approval
and receipt of the complete agreed inputs**. Before kickoff, we agree on the
input list, eligibility rules, coverage cutoff, reviewer, acceptance and
payment terms. A partner may commission the review for one named client
office, with the payer and responsibilities agreed in writing.

The reviewer checks that each recommended action has a reason and a
responsible office role, that missing evidence is marked for verification,
and that the written templates and setup checklist are included. A buyer
can approve the delivered review before the office acts on the worklist.

Qualification, delivery and questions stay in writing. Before transferring
redacted exports, we agree on authorized access, permitted tools and data
retention. Keep customer names, contact details, free-text notes and
credentials out of the initial inquiry.
